Tapca Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game "Tapca" ("the app", "we") handles information. We respect your privacy. The app does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.

Information stored on your device

To let you play, the app saves the following on your own phone only: your game progress (levels, stars, high score), your diamonds, badges and owned planes, theme settings, daily reward streak, a purchase history list, and a randomly generated player ID (UID). This information is not sent to us or to any server. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted from your device.

Purchases

The app sells virtual items (diamonds) through Google Play Billing. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card number or other payment details. Google's handling of your payment information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising and analytics

The app does not show ads and does not use any analytics or tracking tools.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Because the data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Uninstalling also removes your diamonds and progress.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts collecting information in the future (for example, online accounts or leaderboards), we will update this policy before doing so. The latest version will always be available at this page.
